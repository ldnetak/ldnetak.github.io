About Me

Laxman Damodar is a Professor in the Department of Computer Engineering at Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU) , Lonere, India. I am a Professor in the Department of Computer Engineering at Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University, Lonere, Dist. Raigad (MS), India. My research interest in the broad areas of Computer Science and Engineering, such as Geospatial data analysis, Brain-computer interface, Virtual and augmented reality, Machine learning, and Learning analytics. He holds a B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering from Govt. Engineering College, Chatrapati Sambhaji Nagar (Aurangabad) (1995), an M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University, Lonere Raigad (MS) (2003), and a Ph.D. from Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (2016).
